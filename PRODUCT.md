# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

This record covers the New Reality Interactive company site (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`). The Nudge-inator app site has its own record at `nudge-inator/PRODUCT.md`.

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small businesses who are not technical and make the decision themselves. They know their website could do more but don't know where to start, and they're wary of jargon, upselling and agencies that hand them off. They have no particular industry or region. They arrive to decide whether this is someone they can trust with their online presence, and the job they come to do is to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

New Reality Interactive, LLC is a one-person web development and tech consulting business run by Brian. The site explains the services, how working together goes and what it costs, then asks the visitor to start a conversation through the contact form. Success means a qualified small-business owner sends an inquiry.

## Positioning

Agency-calibre engineering from someone with 26 years of software engineering experience, delivered one to one by the person who does the work. The client gets no account managers, no extra vendors and no sales pitch. Brian takes a limited number of clients at a time, gives straight answers about whether something is a good fit, and builds the whole thing himself.

## Operating Context

- Services: custom web development, modernising an existing site, platform and tool integrations (payments, booking, email marketing, CRMs), and tech strategy and advice.
- Process: We Talk (free, no obligation), I Listen, We Build, You Grow (ongoing support).
- Engagements: one-off project work with the scope agreed upfront, or a monthly retainer for ongoing support.
- Inquiry: a modal contact form (name, email, business, what they're looking for, details) posts to Formspree. The site promises a reply within one business day, directly from Brian.
- The footer shows availability ("Available for New Projects" or "Fully Booked Right Now"), toggled by `AVAILABLE` in `js/main.js`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A static single-page site in plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, with no build step, package manager or framework. Hosted on GitHub Pages at newrealityinteractive.com, so merging to `main` publishes it.
- Capacity: the business runs part-time with limited capacity. Every promise about response time, availability or turnaround (the one-business-day reply, retainer "priority availability") must stay sustainable at that capacity. Don't add faster or broader commitments.
- Pricing: $85/hr, no hidden fees, with flexibility when budget is a concern.
- Contact email: contact@newrealityinteractive.com.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is "New Reality Interactive". The legal entity is New Reality Interactive, LLC.
- The voice is first person ("I", not "we"), plain-spoken, warm and honest. It explains without jargon, promises only what is true, and says so when something isn't a fit.
- The founding idea: "technology should tell your story, not just host it."

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: 26 years of experience, the $85/hr rate, the one-business-day reply promise, and a quote from Brian.
- Absent: no client testimonials, case studies, portfolio work, client logos or press. Don't fabricate any of them.
- LinkedIn: the footer links to the Company Page (linkedin.com/company/new-reality-interactive), not Brian's personal profile. Keep it that way.
- Brand assets in `assets/`: the logo lockup, the loop mark, the favicon, the LinkedIn logo and cover, and the Nudge-inator app icon.

## Product Principles

1. Be direct: the visitor deals with the person who does the work, and the site should feel like that person talking.
2. Clarity over impressiveness: someone non-technical should understand every line without help.
3. Be honest about cost and fit, and make no claim the business can't stand behind.
4. Make starting a conversation feel low-risk and easy.
