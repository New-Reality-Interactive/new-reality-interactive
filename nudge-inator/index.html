Nudge-inator

An iPhone app that keeps nudging you about a reminder, the way an iOS Alarm keeps ringing, until you mark it done.

In beta on TestFlight. Coming to the App Store.

Meet the reminder that won't let it go.

No account and no server of ours. Just nudges until you tap Done, and you can share them with someone you trust.

Stop wondering what you forgot.

Whatever's due sits at the top and keeps nudging until you tap Done. Reminders someone sends you nudge the same way.

Know how your day is going, at a glance.

Done, nudging, missed and left, counted at the top, then every reminder in time order. Step back through past days or ahead up to a week, and filter by tag to see just home, or just work.

See how what you sent is going.

Send a reminder to someone you trust, and the Sent view shows each one, grouped by person, with whether it was delivered, done, paused or missed, when they choose to share it.

Set it up once. It keeps nudging.

A title, a time, how often it repeats. Then choose how hard it pushes. Relentless rings as an alarm, even in silent mode and Focus.

Send a reminder to someone you trust.

Invite a person through iCloud, then pick who gets a reminder. Each gets their own copy, at the strength you chose, and it won't nudge you.

Keep your reminders in order.

Tags are one-word labels for filtering. See how many reminders each holds, search them, and tap one to see its reminders, rename it or delete it.

Even Relentless takes the night off.

Set quiet hours, such as 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM every day, or turn on Quiet Now, and no nudge arrives, alarms included. Held nudges aren't sent later. They count as ones you didn't answer.
