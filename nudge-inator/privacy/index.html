Privacy Policy

Nudge-inator is an iPhone app that reminds you of things until you mark them done. This page says what happens to your information.

Last updated November 15, 2026

The short version

  • Nudge-inator collects no data about you.
  • Your own reminders stay on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on Sharing and connect with someone, the reminders you send each other go through your iCloud. Nudge-inator has no server, and New Reality Interactive can’t read them.

What Nudge-inator collects

Nothing. The app has no account and no server. It has no ads and no analytics. It does not track you. New Reality Interactive never receives your reminders, your contacts or anything else from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your own reminders, tags and history are saved on your iPhone. They do not sync to other devices, and they never go to iCloud.

They stay there until you delete them. You can delete one reminder at a time. You can also delete everything at once (see “How to delete your data” below).

Your reminders are part of your own iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the backup can include them. Apple controls those backups, not Nudge-inator. You manage them in your iPhone’s settings.

Sharing reminders through iCloud

Sharing is off until you turn it on in Nudge-inator’s Settings. While it’s off, the app sends nothing new to iCloud, apart from finishing any disconnects. It asks iCloud nothing until you open Connections or accept an invite.

When you connect with someone, the reminders you send each other go through Apple’s iCloud, using your own iCloud account and theirs. Nothing goes through a server of ours. iCloud keeps:

  • the reminders you send: title, notes, due time, repeat, strength, pace and Break through Focus, and, when you change one, what changed;
  • for a reminder you received, whether it reached your iPhone and started nudging or is waiting for you to accept it, and its status (such as Done, Paused or Missed), unless you turn off status sharing for that person or turn on Don’t share this one for that reminder;
  • your choices for each connection, such as status sharing.

Only you and the person you’re connected with can see these. The app stores them in encrypted fields in iCloud, so New Reality Interactive can’t read them. iCloud’s sharing also shows each of you the other’s name, as their Apple Account gives it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy.

To invite someone, you give the email address or phone number of their Apple Account. The app sends it to iCloud so iCloud can find their account and send them the invite.

Contacts

You can type an address yourself, or choose the person from your contacts. The app reads your contacts only in these ways:

  • The contact picker. iOS shows your contacts, and the app receives only the one you choose.
  • The contact access button. When you type part of a name, iOS can offer contacts that match. The app can read a contact only after you choose to share it this way.
  • Full access. If you give Nudge-inator full access to your contacts in your iPhone’s Settings, it searches them for names that match what you type, to suggest who to invite.

Your contacts stay on your iPhone. The only part that leaves it is the address of the person you invite, which goes to iCloud as described above.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Nudge-inator.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete All Data, then tap Delete.

This deletes every reminder, tag and all history from your iPhone. It also cancels every nudge and alarm. If you have connections, it also disconnects everyone and deletes the reminders you sent and received, on your iPhone and in iCloud. It cannot be undone.

You can also end one connection at a time with Disconnect, or turn Sharing off in Nudge-inator’s Settings, which ends every connection.

If you want to remove the app, do this first. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms scheduled on your iPhone. Use Delete All Data first, then delete the app.

Permissions

Nudge-inator asks to send notifications and to set alarms. It uses them only to remind you on your iPhone. It uses your contacts only as described in “Contacts” above. You can turn any of these off in your iPhone’s Settings.

Links

Settings › About has links to this page and to the support page. When you tap one, your iPhone opens your web browser. The page you visit is a web page, not part of the app. You start this yourself, and the app sends nothing when you do.

TestFlight

Some people try Nudge-inator before its release through Apple’s TestFlight. TestFlight is Apple’s service, not Nudge-inator’s. If you use it:

  • Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information, and shares them with the developer. You cannot turn this off.
  • If you send feedback from TestFlight, the developer receives it. Feedback can include your comments, screenshots, and details about your device, such as its model, iOS version, carrier, time zone and connection type. Apple says it may be linked to your name and email address.
  • A screenshot shows whatever is on your screen, including your reminder text. Crop it or choose a different screen before you send it.
  • The developer does not share this information with anyone else.

Children

Nudge-inator collects no data from anyone, including children.
