Nudge-inator

Privacy Policy

Nudge-inator has no account and no server. Your reminders stay on your iPhone and in your own backups.

Last updated October 25, 2026

What the app stores

Nudge-inator stores the reminders you create: the title, the schedule, the strength (Gentle, Firm or Relentless), the pace, and whether each one is Done, nudging, paused or missed. That's all it keeps.

Where your reminders live

Reminders are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's local database. iCloud sync is turned off, so they aren't copied to Apple's servers or to us.

Your reminders are included in your own iPhone backups, to iCloud or to a computer, the same way iOS backs up any app's data. Those backups are the only other copy.

What we don't collect

The app makes no network requests. It includes no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”, and its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

Nudges and alarms

Nudges are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. For reminders that break through Focus, nudges are system alarms, also scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Reminders stay on your iPhone until you delete them. To remove everything, go to Settings › Delete All Data. It removes every reminder and cancels every nudge and alarm.

Use Delete All Data before you delete the app. Deleting the app alone leaves its alarms scheduled. Copies in your backups last as long as you keep those backups.

Third parties

The app sends no data anywhere, so no third party receives any data from it.

Beta testing with TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's app for beta testing, and it isn't part of Nudge-inator. When you test a beta, Apple shares crash logs and usage information with us, along with any feedback you send: comments, screenshots, and device details such as the model, iOS version and free disk space.

We use that information only to improve the app, and we don't share it with anyone. A screenshot can show your reminder text, so send only what you're comfortable sharing. To ask for your TestFlight data or have it deleted, write to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, this page is updated in the same release, and the date at the top changes.
