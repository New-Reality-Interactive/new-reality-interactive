# Carousel images: source

The six `carousel-*.png` images are drawn from the Nudge-inator iOS mockup 1.3.0
(`docs/mockups/1.3.0/index.html`, frozen), rendered in one pass in headless Chrome.
Each is the first page of its screen, 600 by 1302 pixels (a 393 by 852 pt screen drawn at 3x, then
resized). The mockup's clock is fixed at Monday, Sep 28, 2026, 8:20 AM, and its data is made up.

Rendered 2026-11-09 with `scripts/mockup-shots.py` from the Nudge-inator checkout at `633fe5f`,
with the window set to the screen's size and the New Reminder form filled in as approved in #28
(Pick up the kids from practice, #home, 5:30 PM, Every Week, Relentless, Consistent every 10 min).
`carousel-contact-sheet.png` shows the six side by side.
